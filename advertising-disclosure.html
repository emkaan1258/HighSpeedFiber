1. Who We Are

HighSpeedFiberInternet.com is an independent comparison platform and authorized independent sales agent operated by Empiric Resource Agency LLC, a New York limited liability company located at 18 Prospect ST, Hicksville, NY 11801.

We are NOT an internet service provider (ISP). We are NOT a cable TV company. We are NOT a telecommunications carrier. We do not own, operate, manage, or maintain any network infrastructure, equipment, or service lines.

We operate as an authorized independent sales agent through master distributor agreements with:

  • DSI Systems
  • Perfect Vision
  • AllConnect

These master distributors hold direct contracts with major U.S. residential internet service providers, which allows us to facilitate plan comparisons and enrollment with these providers.

3. How We Are Compensated

High Speed Fiber Internet, operated by Empiric Resource Agency LLC, may receive compensation in the following ways:

3.1 Referral Commissions

When a customer calls our number or submits an enrollment request through our website and subsequently enrolls in a service plan with a participating provider, Empiric Resource Agency LLC may receive a referral commission or sales fee from the service provider.

3.2 Affiliate Program Participation

We participate in authorized affiliate and reseller programs with the master distributors listed in Section 1.

3.3 Important Compensation Facts

  • Compensation is paid by the service provider — NOT by you
  • Compensation does NOT affect the price you pay for the service
  • Compensation does NOT add any fees to your monthly bill
  • Compensation does NOT affect which providers we show you
  • All pricing you see is set entirely by the provider per their published terms

Our compensation model means our services are provided at no cost to consumers. You will never receive a bill, fee, or charge from Empiric Resource Agency LLC for using our comparison or enrollment assistance services.

4. How We Advertise

We purchase paid advertising on various platforms to reach consumers actively searching for or interested in comparing internet and TV service plans. Our advertising platforms include:

  • Google Ads
  • Meta Ads (Facebook and Instagram)
  • Microsoft Advertising (Bing)
  • Other digital advertising networks

4.1 What Our Advertising Targets

Our advertisements target consumers who are:

  • Actively searching for internet service plans by ZIP code or city
  • Comparing internet service providers
  • Researching internet plans for new homes or moves
  • Looking for bundle options combining internet, TV, and home phone
  • Searching for specific provider plans (with clear disclosure that we are not the provider)

4.2 What Our Advertising Does NOT Target

We do NOT run advertisements that target:

  • Existing customer service queries for specific providers
  • Technical support queries (e.g., “AT&T tech support phone number”)
  • Billing or account management queries
  • Cancellation or downgrade queries
  • Customers seeking to contact a specific provider directly

4.3 Ad Disclosure Practices

All advertisements we publish clearly identify High Speed Fiber Internet as an authorized independent sales agent — not the provider. Our ads and landing pages prominently display our identity and our role.

5. How We Display Information

We strive to provide accurate, current, and helpful information about internet plans. Our practices include:

5.1 Plan Information Sources

All plan details we display on our website are sourced from:

  • Each provider’s official published terms and pricing
  • Each provider’s FCC Broadband Facts Labels (where applicable)
  • Direct communications with our master distributor partners
  • Industry-standard plan comparison databases

5.2 Pricing Disclaimers

All pricing shown on our website is presented as estimates based on each provider’s currently published rates. We use “Est.” or “Estimated” prefixes and “as of [date]” anchors to clarify pricing timing. Actual pricing may vary depending on:

  • Service address availability
  • Applicable taxes and fees
  • Equipment selections
  • Contract terms (where applicable)
  • Promotional eligibility (AutoPay, paperless billing, new customer status, etc.)
  • Current provider promotions

We strongly encourage customers to verify all current pricing directly with the selected provider before enrolling. Links to each provider’s official FCC Broadband Facts Labels are provided for verification.

5.3 Provider Selection

We display providers in our network based on:

  • Service address availability (ZIP code-based filtering)
  • Plan compatibility with customer needs
  • Provider participation in our master distributor agreements

We do NOT receive higher compensation based on which provider you select. Our compensation structure is uniform across our provider network.

6. Editorial Independence

While we maintain commercial relationships with the providers in our network, our presentation of information is intended to be:

  • Factual and based on each provider’s published terms
  • Free of editorialized rankings or “best” claims
  • Inclusive of all participating providers available at your address
  • Transparent about provider strengths and limitations

We do NOT make subjective claims about providers being “best,” “perfect,” “tailored,” or “right for you.” All such determinations are personal choices that depend on your specific household needs, service address, and preferences.

7. Limitations of Our Role

Our role is strictly limited to:

7.1 What We DO:

  • Answer general questions about plan features and pricing

7.2 What We Do NOT Do:
