📋 Important Bundle Information

Advertised Pricing: Prices shown are estimates from each provider’s published rates as of October 2026. Bundle pricing may require conditions such as AutoPay, paperless billing, minimum service tiers, bundling multiple services, and/or contract terms. Taxes, equipment, installation, Broadcast TV Surcharge, Regional Sports Fees, and other charges may apply unless stated otherwise.

Internet Speeds: Speeds shown are maximum advertised speeds under ideal conditions. Actual speeds vary by network conditions, equipment, time of day, and device limits. Wired speeds are typically faster than Wi-Fi. Fiber plans typically offer symmetrical upload/download speeds.

Channel Counts: Channel counts are ranges and may vary by package tier and market. Some channels may be unavailable in certain areas due to regional restrictions and licensing.

Service Availability: Plans and bundles differ by service address and provider footprint. Always verify the exact bundle offer, total monthly cost, and included equipment directly with the provider for your location.

Contract Terms: Some offers may require a 12-24 month agreement and may include early termination fees. Month-to-month options may be available at different price points.
