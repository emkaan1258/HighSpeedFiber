Cable TV vs. Streaming TV

Cable TV offers traditional live programming through dedicated cable infrastructure with a set-top box. Fiber TV (offered by some fiber internet providers) uses fiber optic infrastructure for TV service. Streaming TV provides flexibility through apps but relies on your internet connection quality. Each option has different equipment, channel availability, and pricing.
