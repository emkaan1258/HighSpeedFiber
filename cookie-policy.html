This Cookie Policy explains how Empiric Resource Agency LLC (“we,” “us,” “Highspeedfiberinternet.com”) uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on our website.

For more information on how we handle your personal data, please review our Privacy Policy.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile) when you visit a website. They help the site remember your actions and preferences (such as language, region, and login information) over time, so you don’t have to re-enter them on each visit.

We also use similar tracking technologies including:

  • Web Beacons: Tiny graphics that track user behavior
  • Pixel Tags: Used for advertising conversion tracking
  • Local Storage: Browser-based data storage
  • Session Storage: Temporary data during your visit

2. Types of Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies (Always Active)

  • Required for basic site functionality
  • Enable navigation, security, and accessibility features
  • Cannot be disabled
  • Examples: Session cookies, security tokens

Functional Cookies

  • Remember your choices (language, region, ZIP code)
  • Enhance your browsing experience
  • Personalize content display

Advertising & Marketing Cookies

  • Used to deliver relevant advertisements
  • Measure ad campaign performance
  • Track conversions and ROI
  • Example provider: Google Ads

3. Cookie Duration

Cookies on our site fall into two categories:

  • Session Cookies: Deleted when you close your browser
  • Persistent Cookies: Remain on your device for a set period (typically 30 days to 24 months)

Specific durations vary by cookie type and provider. Advertising cookies typically expire in 30-90 days.

4. Third-Party Cookies

We allow a third-party provider to place cookies on your device for advertising. This currently includes:

  • Google Ads — Advertising and conversion tracking

This third party may also collect information about your browsing activities across different websites. Its data practices are governed by its own privacy policy, not ours.

5. How to Manage Cookies

You have several options to control or delete cookies:

Browser Settings:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

Industry Opt-Out Tools:

  • Network Advertising Initiative (NAI)
  • Digital Advertising Alliance (DAA)
  • European Digital Advertising Alliance (EDAA)

Note: Disabling cookies may affect site functionality, including ZIP code lookup, form submissions, and personalized features.

6. “Do Not Track” Signals

Our website currently does not respond to “Do Not Track” browser signals due to lack of industry consensus on implementation. We will update this policy if standards change.

7. Global Privacy Control (GPC)

We honor Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals as opt-out requests for the sharing of personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, as required by California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA).

8. Your Privacy Rights

California Residents (CCPA/CPRA):

  • Right to opt-out of “sale” or “sharing” of personal information
  • Right to limit use of sensitive personal information
  • Right to non-discrimination for exercising privacy rights

To exercise your rights, contact us at info@highspeedfiberinternet.com.

9. Cookie Choices

We do not currently show a cookie banner. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and we treat a Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal as an opt-out of sharing for advertising.

To opt out of sharing by other means, see Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information.
