Astound Broadband Internet Options for Your Home

Astound Broadband is a regional cable and fiber internet, TV, and home phone provider serving parts of 10 states and Washington, D.C. Plans commonly range from about 300 Mbps up to 1.5 Gig depending on address, with no annual contract on standard residential internet offers. In some markets Astound operates as Astound powered by RCN, Wave Broadband, Grande Communications, or En-Touch. Plan availability, speeds, equipment, and pricing vary by service address per Astound’s currently published terms.

No Data Caps

Astound residential internet plans typically do not include data caps, so everyday streaming, browsing, and device use are not subject to a monthly data limit — subject to Astound’s acceptable use policies.

No Annual Contracts

Standard Astound internet plans generally require no long-term contract for introductory pricing. Optional price-lock or Price for Life offers may be available on eligible tiers depending on market.

Speeds Up to Multi-Gig

Where the network reaches you, Astound offers tiers from about 300 Mbps up to 1.5 Gig — suitable for multi-device homes, streaming, and remote work, subject to plan and network conditions.
