WOW! Internet Options for Your Home

WOW! (WideOpenWest) is a residential internet provider offering cable and fiber plans in select markets across Alabama, Florida, Georgia, Michigan, South Carolina, and Tennessee. Cable tiers commonly start around 300 Mbps, with multi-gig options where the network reaches you, and symmetrical fiber is available in select fiber markets. Plan availability, speeds, equipment, and pricing vary by service address per WOW!’s currently published terms.

No Data Caps on Standard Plans

Standard residential WOW! internet plans typically include unlimited data and no annual contract, so you can change plans without early termination fees — subject to WOW!’s standard terms.

Cable & Fiber Options

Choose cable speeds for everyday streaming and gaming, or symmetrical fiber where available for stronger uploads — ideal for video calls, remote work, and multi-device homes.

Clear AutoPay Pricing

Advertised rates often require AutoPay in select markets. Optional Price Lock for Life and Whole-Home WiFi (eero) add-ons may be available depending on plan and location.
