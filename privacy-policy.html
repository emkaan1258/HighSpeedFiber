Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Empiric Resource Agency LLC (“we,” “us,” “Highspeedfiberinternet.com”) collects, uses, and safeguards your personal information when you visit our website or interact with our services.

Highspeedfiberinternet.com is operated as an authorized independent sales agent through master distributor agreements with DSI Systems, Perfect Vision, and AllConnect. We are not an internet service provider.

1. Information We Collect

Personal Information You Provide:

  • Name, email address, phone number
  • Service address (street, city, state, ZIP code)
  • Communication preferences
  • Service plan selections

Automatically Collected:

  • IP address and approximate location
  • Browser type, device, and operating system
  • Pages visited and timestamps
  • Referring website

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Process ZIP code lookups and display available plans
  • Facilitate enrollment with selected service providers
  • Respond to inquiries and provide customer support
  • Send service-related communications
  • Send marketing communications (with consent)
  • Analyze website usage and improve user experience
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Detect and prevent fraud

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share with:

  • Service Providers: AT&T, Verizon, Xfinity, Frontier, Optimum, Brightspeed, Metronet, Kinetic by Windstream (only when you enroll)
  • Master Distributors: DSI Systems, Perfect Vision, AllConnect (to facilitate enrollment)
  • Trusted Vendors: Hosting, analytics, marketing platforms (under confidentiality agreements)
  • Legal Authorities: When required by law

4. Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies for:

  • Essential website functionality
  • Advertising conversion tracking (Google Ads)
  • Remembering preferences

You can manage cookies through browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect site functionality.

We call or text you only if you give us permission by checking an unchecked consent box on a form, or by asking an agent to call you back. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Reply STOP to stop texts, or tell us on any call.

6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including SSL/TLS encryption, secure storage, and access controls. However, no method of transmission is 100% secure. We will notify you of any data breaches per applicable laws.

7. Your Privacy Rights

All Users: Access, correct, delete, or restrict use of your personal information.

California Residents (CCPA/CPRA):

  • Right to know what we collect
  • Right to delete personal information
  • Right to correct inaccurate information
  • Right to opt-out of sharing
  • Right to non-discrimination
  • Right to data portability

Do Not Sell or Share: See section 10 below.

Nevada Residents: Right to opt-out of sale of covered information (we do not sell).

EU/EEA Residents (GDPR): Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and right to withdraw consent.

To exercise your rights: Contact us at info@highspeedfiberinternet.com or (855) 661-6305.

8. Children’s Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us immediately for deletion.

Our site may link to third-party websites (provider sites, FCC.gov, social media). We are not responsible for their privacy practices. Review their policies before providing information.

10. Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information

We do not sell your personal information. Our website uses an advertising tag (Google Ads) that may be considered “sharing” for cross-context behavioral advertising under California law. To opt out, email info@highspeedfiberinternet.com with the subject “Do Not Share,” or call (855) 661-6305 and ask us not to share your information. We also treat a Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal from your browser as an opt-out of sharing for advertising.
